Splice

    How to play

    Put the words in place so that every word and the one under it make a word or a familiar phrase.

    FIREWORKmakes firework
    FIREALARMmakes fire alarm

    Tap two words to swap them. Sure of a pair? Tap the branch between them to mark it amber, and the two move together. Tap it again to let go.

    When you think the tree is right, Check. Each link turns green when both of its words are where they belong, and green words stay put.

    You get four checks Monday to Thursday and three from Friday, when the trees are biggest. There’s only one way to finish each tree.

    A new tree arrives every midnight. Want more? Practice deals as many as you like.
