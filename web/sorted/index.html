How to play

Sixteen words hide four groups of four. Tap four that belong together and press Check.

A right group is set aside. A wrong one costs one of your four mistakes, and if three of your four belonged together you’ll see One away.

Plenty of words seem to fit two groups, but only one way works for all sixteen.
