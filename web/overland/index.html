Overland

How to play

Get from the yellow country to the blue one by land. Name the countries in between, in any order, and each one appears on the map.

PortugalSpainFranceGermany
Spain and France join Portugal to Germany, so that's two guesses.

A guess turns green once a border joins it to either end. You win when the two ends are joined, and you only get a few guesses more than the shortest route needs.

The map shows no borders, so you need to know what's next to what. Only land borders count: no bridges or tunnels. Spain and Morocco touch at Ceuta and Melilla, Russia reaches Poland and Lithuania at Kaliningrad, and France means France in Europe.

Pinch or drag the map to look around, or double-tap to zoom in. Fit brings the whole route back. Once the game is over, the whole map opens up to explore.

The week gets harder. Monday's route needs 3 countries, with 3 guesses to spare. By Sunday it's 7 or 8, with 2 to spare.

A new route arrives every midnight. Want more? Practice deals as many as you like.
