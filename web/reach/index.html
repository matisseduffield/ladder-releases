    How to play

    Make the target from the six numbers. Tap a number, an operator, then a second number, and the answer takes their place. You don’t have to use them all.

    Tap 7, +, 6 and you have a 13 to use next.

    Numbers stay whole and above zero, so 7 ÷ 2 and 5 − 5 won’t work. Tap the two in either order: the bigger one goes first.

    Par is the fewest moves it can possibly be done in, so it can be matched but never beaten. It’s 3 on Monday and Tuesday, then 4. The weekend is hardest, with very few ways to match it.

    A new puzzle arrives every midnight. Want more? Practice deals as many as you like.
