Cluey

How to play

A small crossword every day. Fill every square so each row and column of letters answers its clue.

OWLROEBET
1 Across: Night shift worker?
The answer is OWL, not a nurse. The crossing letters tell you which.
  • Tap a square to pick it, and tap it again to turn between across and down.
  • The clue sits above the keyboard. Tap it or use the arrows to change clue.
  • A clue ending in ? is having you on: think of another meaning.

Monday is 5×5 and plain. The clues get slyer each day, Friday is 6×6, and the weekend is 7×7 and hardest.

Check marks the word you’re on, and Reveal fills in the square you’re on. You get three of each. A clean solve uses neither and is right the first time the grid is full, because being told it’s wrong counts too.
