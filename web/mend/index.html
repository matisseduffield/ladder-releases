Mend

How to play

Every line of tiles is a real word, but the letters have been mixed up. Put them back before your swaps run out. Tap one tile and then another to swap them, or drag one onto the other. A swap can’t be undone.

The words are TOE and GOAT. Swap the top A with the yellow T in the row and both go green. The E and the last T are now grey: neither is wanted in its own word, so each belongs in the other’s. One more swap finishes it.

A word only lights as many of a letter as it still needs. If a word shows two Es and wants one more, one is yellow and one grey. A grey tile always means its words already show every one they need.

Each puzzle has one solution, and you get three swaps more than the fewest it can be done in. What’s left at the end is your score. The week gets harder: bigger boards, fewer tiles in place and trickier openings, some showing a real word in the wrong place.

A new puzzle arrives every midnight. Want more? Practice deals as many as you like.
