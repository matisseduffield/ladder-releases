Slugle

How to play

Every day there’s a new word to start from, and every ladder ends on SLUG. Change one letter at a time, and make every step a real word.

FROG to SLUG in three steps

1

Type the next word and press Enter. It has to be the word above with one letter changed.

2

Par is the fewest steps it can be done in. Gentle on Monday, longest on Sunday.

3

Green letters are already where they are in SLUG. Stuck? Hint marks the letter to change; tap it again for the word.

4

Tap an earlier word to go back to it. A new ladder arrives every midnight; missed one? Tap the date.

5

Want more? Practice deals as many as you like, short, medium or long.
