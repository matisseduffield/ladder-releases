How to play

Put each event in its place on the timeline. Tap a gap, or drag the yellow card there. Its year stays hidden until you place it.

1876Alexander Graham Bell patents the telephone
Alexander Bain patents a fax machine
Before or after the telephone? Tap Later and you lose a life, because the fax came first:
1843Alexander Bain patents a fax machine
1876Alexander Graham Bell patents the telephone

A wrong card slides to its true place and stays there to help you. You have three lives, and the game ends when every card is placed or the lives are gone. Your score is the cards you got right and the lives you kept.

The week gets harder. Monday has 6 well-known events decades apart. By Sunday there are 10, packed within a few years of each other, and some will surprise you.

A new game arrives every midnight. Want more? Practice deals as many as you like.
